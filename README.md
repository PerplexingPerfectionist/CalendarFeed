## Subscribing in Google Calendar

1. Open Google Calendar
2. Click the **+** next to "Other calendars"
3. Choose **"From URL"**
4. Paste the feed URL
5. Click **Add calendar**

## Subscribing on iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1. Open **Settings** → **Calendar** → **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Other** → **Add Subscribed Calendar**
2. Paste the feed URL in the **Server** field
3. Tap **Next**, then **Save**

Or via the Calendar app:
1. Open **Calendar** → tap **Calendars** (bottom center)
2. Tap **Add Calendar** → **Add Subscription Calendar**
3. Paste the feed URL → tap **Subscribe** → **Add**
